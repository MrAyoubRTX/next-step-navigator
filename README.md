# 🌍 Vision

NextStep is a startup prototype built around a simple belief:

> Every young person deserves access to the right guidance, opportunities, and support to build a successful future.

Instead of forcing students and graduates to navigate multiple disconnected platforms, NextStep centralizes academic orientation, recruitment services, and entrepreneurial support into a single digital ecosystem.

---

# ❓ The Problem

Every year thousands of talented young people face the same challenges:

🎓 Choosing the wrong academic path.

💼 Difficulty finding internships and jobs.

📉 High graduate unemployment.

🚀 Lack of support for turning ideas into businesses.

Existing platforms usually solve only one of these problems.

NextStep connects the entire journey.

---

# 💡 The Solution

NextStep combines three essential services into one platform:

## 🎯 Academic & Career Orientation

Helping students choose the right educational and professional path through personalized guidance.

---

## 💼 Internship & Recruitment

Connecting graduates with companies while helping them prepare CVs, interviews, and career strategies.

---

## 🚀 Entrepreneurship Support

Helping aspiring entrepreneurs transform ideas into structured and viable businesses with expert mentoring.
