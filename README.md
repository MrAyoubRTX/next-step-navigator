---

> ## 🚧 Prototype Notice
>
> **NextStep is the first functional MVP (Minimum Viable Product) of a real-world startup concept.**
>
> This prototype was developed to validate the product vision, user experience, technical architecture, and business model before scaling into a production-ready platform.
>
> Rather than building another academic CRUD application, the objective was to design a solution capable of addressing real challenges faced by students, graduates, companies, and aspiring entrepreneurs in Morocco.
>
> The MVP demonstrates the platform's core workflows, validates market assumptions, and establishes a scalable foundation for future iterations, AI-powered features, and enterprise integrations.
